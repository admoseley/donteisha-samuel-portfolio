01

Onboarding Workshop for New Instructional Designers

Concept projectADDIEWorkshop designFacilitation
ProblemNew instructional designers often ramp up inconsistently, with no shared foundation in design process.
ApproachDesigned a structured workshop walking through each ADDIE phase with hands-on practice, templates, and aligned objectives.
Design rationaleGrounded each phase in adult-learning principles so new designers build confidence through practice rather than lecture.
Success measuresIf implemented, I'd track time-to-first-deliverable, pre/post confidence self-assessments, and manager feedback at 30/60/90 days.

02

My Future, My Fit: Instructional Materials & Assessment

Concept projectCurriculumAssessmentBackward design
ProblemHigh-school students need a clear way to explore college, certification, apprenticeship, and workforce pathways.
ApproachDesigned instructional materials plus an aligned assessment that guide students to compare options and build a personalized post-graduation plan.
Design rationaleUsed backward design so every activity ties to a clear objective, with accessible, low-jargon materials suited to a teen audience.
Success measuresIf used in a classroom, I'd look at assessment completion, the quality of students' pathway plans, and self-reported clarity about next steps.

03

Implementation & Evaluation Reflection

Concept projectEvaluationKirkpatrickIteration
ProblemOnce a learning solution is delivered, how do you know it's actually working for learners?
ApproachBuilt an evaluation framework examining delivery, alignment to objectives, and learner engagement, then mapped concrete revisions.
Design rationaleApplied Kirkpatrick-style thinking to separate "did they like it" from "did they learn it," so revisions target real gaps.
Success measuresI'd evaluate against the learning objectives using completion, assessment scores, and learner feedback to prioritize the highest-impact fixes.
