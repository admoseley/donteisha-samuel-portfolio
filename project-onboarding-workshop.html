Workshop Design · ADDIE

Onboarding Workshop for New Instructional Designers

A five-day workshop grounding new ID team members in design history, foundational theory, emerging trends, and the full ADDIE framework — built for practice, not just lecture.

DS
D. Samuel • April 20–25, 2026 • Concept project

New instructional designers often enter teams with inconsistent foundations — strong in some areas, uncertain in others. This workshop addresses that gap with a structured, five-day onboarding experience that moves participants from the roots of the field through practical application of the ADDIE model. Presented by Dr. Don'Teisha Samuel at Instructional Design Co., Conference Room B.

Theory without application is trivia. The goal is to use these frameworks to make better decisions — not to cite them.

Workshop Overview

Session 1 — Introduction to Instructional Design

Session 2 — Major Theorists and Their Impact

Session 3 — Current Trends in Instructional Design

Session 4 — The ADDIE Model
